Catalyst.
Privacy Policy · Last updated September 2026

Catalyst is presented by Saksham — the Entrepreneurship Club at IIM Udaipur. This page explains what information the Catalyst app and website collect, how it's used, and how you can control it.

What we collect

If you just read Catalyst without signing in, we don't collect anything about you personally.

If you choose to sign in with Google (to save bookmarks), we receive the following from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo

We also store whatever you choose to bookmark — news articles, startups, founders, or vocabulary terms you save to read later — tied to your account.

How we use it

Your Google account information is used only to identify you so your bookmarks are yours alone, and to show your name/photo back to you in the app. Your bookmarks exist only to be shown back to you in the Bookmarks tab, across sessions and devices.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with any third party for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Your account information and bookmarks are stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), Google's own infrastructure for app data. Firebase's own privacy practices apply to how it handles data on our behalf: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your choices

  • You can remove any individual bookmark at any time, either by tapping its heart again or removing it directly from the Bookmarks tab.
  • You can sign out at any time from the app.
  • You can request that we delete your account data entirely by contacting us at the email below — we'll remove your stored bookmarks and account record.

Children's privacy

Catalyst is intended for an MBA/university student and alumni audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Catalyst after a change means you accept the updated policy.
